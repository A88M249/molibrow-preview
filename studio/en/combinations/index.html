FAQ

Good to know

How much does a combination cost?

Combinations start from 1400 NOK: lash lift & brow shaping 1400, lash lift & brow lift 1500, brow lamination & lash lift without tint 1550, and lash lift & brow lamination 1750.

How long does a combination take?

From 1 hour 45 minutes to 2 hours 30 minutes, depending on the combination.

Can I skip the tint?

Yes – brow lamination & lash lift without tint (1550 NOK) is for naturally dark hairs or a completely natural look.

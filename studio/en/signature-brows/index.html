What are signature brows?

Signature brows are a tailored brow treatment: I map your brows to your face, shape them and tint them in a shade that suits your hair colour.

The tint lasts 4–10 days on the skin and 4–5 weeks on the hairs. The result is brows with clearer shape and colour that still look natural.

Good for you if

  • You want clearer shape and colour in your brows.
  • You want brows tailored to your face – not a template.
  • You want a simpler treatment than lamination (60 min).

Before your visit

  • Avoid self-tanner for at least 7 days before your appointment.
  • Avoid skincare with active ingredients (vitamin C, retinol, acids/peels) for the 7 days before.
  • Minimum age: 16.
Treatments are not done on broken or irritated skin, active skin conditions or an eye infection. Unsure? Get in touch before you book.

FAQ

Good to know

How long do signature brows last?

The tint lasts 4–10 days on the skin and 4–5 weeks on the hairs.

How much do signature brows cost?

Signature brows – shape & tint – cost 650 NOK and take 60 minutes.

What's the difference between signature brows and brow lamination?

Signature brows are about mapping, shaping and tint. Brow lamination lifts and sets the hairs for a softer, fuller look. They can be combined – talk to Lina at the consultation.

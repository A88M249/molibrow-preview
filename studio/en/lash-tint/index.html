What is a lash tint?

A tint gives the lashes a darker colour, so they look fuller and more defined without mascara. It's a quick treatment for when you want a little more definition.

Want both lift and colour? Lash lift & tint (1100 NOK) is a great choice.

Good for you if

  • You want darker lashes without mascara.
  • You want a quick treatment (30 min).
  • You want to add tint to a lash lift.

Before your visit

  • Please arrive without mascara.
  • Avoid self-tanner and skincare with active ingredients (vitamin C, retinol, acids) for the 7 days before.
  • Minimum age: 16.
Treatments are not done on broken or irritated skin, active skin conditions or an eye infection. Unsure? Get in touch before you book.

What is brow shaping?

Shaping gives your brows a clean, balanced shape that suits your face. I map them out, remove stray hairs with wax and tweezers, and shape them precisely.

Shaping can be booked on its own, or combined with a lash lift – see combinations.

Good for you if

  • You want tidy, defined brows.
  • Women and men.
  • You want to keep the shape between other brow treatments.

Before your visit

  • Avoid self-tanner for at least 7 days before your appointment.
  • Avoid skincare with active ingredients (vitamin C, retinol, acids/peels) for the 7 days before.
  • Minimum age: 16.
Treatments are not done on broken or irritated skin, active skin conditions or an eye infection. Unsure? Get in touch before you book.

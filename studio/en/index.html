FAQ

Good to know.

Can't find the answer? Send an email – or ask at your appointment.

How do I prepare?

Avoid self-tanner and skincare with active ingredients (vitamin C, retinol, acids) for 7 days before. Arrive without mascara for lash treatments.

How long do results last?

Lash lift 6–8 weeks, brow lamination 4–6 weeks and signature brows 4–5 weeks on the hairs.

Is it right for me?

We decide that in the consultation. Treatments aren't done on broken or irritated skin, active skin conditions or eye infection. Minimum age: 16.

How do I cancel?

Cancel in Timma at least 24 hours before. Later cancellations or no-shows may be charged 100%.

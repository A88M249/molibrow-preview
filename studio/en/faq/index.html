FAQ

Good to know

How do I prepare?

Avoid self-tanner and skincare with active ingredients (vitamin C, retinol, acids) for 7 days before. Arrive without mascara for lash treatments.

How long do results last?

Lash lift 6–8 weeks, brow lamination 4–6 weeks and signature brows 4–5 weeks on the hairs (the tint lasts 4–10 days on the skin).

Is the treatment right for me?

We decide that in the consultation. Treatments aren't done on broken or irritated skin, active skin conditions or an eye infection.

Is there a minimum age?

Yes, the minimum age is 16.

How do I cancel?

Cancel in Timma at least 24 hours before. Later cancellations or no-shows may be charged 100% of the price.

How do I book?

You book securely online via Timma. Choose a treatment and a time that suits you.

Is there parking?

Yes, there is free parking right outside the studio at Sundgata 7.

Can I buy a gift card?

Yes – gift cards of 500 NOK, 750 NOK or any amount. Choose “Gift cards” in the booking.

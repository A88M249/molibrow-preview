What is a Korean lash lift?

A lash lift gives your own lashes lift and a soft curl, without extensions. The Korean technique suits both short and long lashes, and lashes that are straight or grow downward.

Choose a lash lift with tint for more definition, or without tint if your lashes are already dark or you want a completely natural result. Arrive without mascara and you're set.

Good for you if

  • You want lifted lashes without extensions.
  • You have short or long, straight or downward-growing lashes.
  • You want to save time on curlers and mascara every day.
  • No tint suits naturally dark lashes.

Before your visit

  • Please arrive without mascara.
  • Avoid self-tanner and skincare with active ingredients (vitamin C, retinol, acids) for the 7 days before.
  • Minimum age: 16.
Treatments are not done on broken or irritated skin, active skin conditions or an eye infection. Unsure? Get in touch before you book.

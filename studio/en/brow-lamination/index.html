What is brow lamination?

Brow lamination lifts the brow hairs and sets them in a more even direction, so brows look softer, fuller and are easier to style. You can choose a fluffy look or something more structured.

At Molí Brow Studio we always start with a consultation. I look at your hairs and your wishes before we begin, and we agree on shape and look together. Then comes lamination, mapping, shaping and tint – with every step explained as we go.

Good for you if

  • You want softer, fuller brows with more structure.
  • You want brows that are easier to style every day.
  • You want a natural look shaped to your face – not to a template.
  • No tint suits naturally dark hairs, or a completely natural result.

Before your visit

  • Avoid self-tanner for at least 7 days before your appointment.
  • Avoid skincare with active ingredients (vitamin C, retinol, acids/peels) for the 7 days before.
  • Minimum age: 16.
Treatments are not done on broken or irritated skin, active skin conditions or an eye infection. Unsure? Get in touch before you book.

FAQ

Good to know

Is brow lamination right for me?

Brow lamination isn't for everyone, so I always start with a consultation where we look at your hairs and wishes. It isn't done on broken or irritated skin, active skin conditions or an eye infection.

How long does brow lamination last?

Brow lamination usually lasts 4–6 weeks. Lina gives you personal aftercare advice at your appointment.

How much does brow lamination cost in Eidsvoll?

Brow lamination costs 1000 NOK (90 min). Without tint it costs 900 NOK (75 min). All prices are fixed and in NOK.

Can I combine it with a lash lift?

Yes. Lash lift & brow lamination costs 1750 NOK (150 min), or 1550 NOK without tint (105 min).
